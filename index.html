About New Gitanjali Hotel

Your Premium Beachside Stay in New Digha

New Gitanjali Hotel is a premium beachside stay located in the heart of New Digha, just a few minutes’ walk from the sea beach. We are known for our modern amenities, prime location, and warm hospitality that make every stay relaxing and memorable. Whether you are travelling with family, as a couple, or with friends, our hotel provides a clean, safe and comfortable environment close to all major attractions.

Our rooms are designed for comfort and convenience, featuring AC, cozy interiors, attached bathrooms, and essential facilities to make your stay stress-free. Guests enjoy complimentary breakfast, high-speed Wi-Fi, 24×7 assistance, and swimming pool access during their stay. We focus on delivering a peaceful resort-like experience at an affordable price.

Our location gives travellers quick access to Amarabati Park, the Marine Aquarium, the beachside promenade, and local markets — perfect for sightseeing and evening strolls. With a blend of modern design, coastal charm, and attentive service, New Gitanjali Hotel ensures a beautiful stay in New Digha.
